# Riverside Public Library, Community Room

**Address:** 88 Mill Road, Bay City
**Capacity:** 60 seated

## Getting there
- **Transit:** Bus 7 stops at Mill Road and 2nd. The nearest train is 15 minutes away on foot.
- **Parking:** Free library lot behind the building, 45 spaces. It fills up by 6 PM on weekdays.
- **Bikes:** Outdoor racks at the front entrance.

## Getting in
- Use the side entrance on 2nd Avenue after 6 PM; the main doors lock when the library closes.
- No check-in desk. A volunteer greets you at the side door.

## Rooms
| Room | Seats | AV |
|---|---|---|
| Community Room | 60 | Ceiling projector (HDMI only), 1 handheld mic |
| Study Room 2 | 8 | Whiteboard only, good for speaker prep |

## Wifi
Network `RiversideLibrary-Public`. No password; accept the terms page.

## Accessibility
- Step-free side entrance with an automatic door.
- Accessible restroom next to the Community Room.
- A hearing loop is installed in the Community Room.

## Food and drink
- Library rules: covered drinks only in the Community Room. Food is served in the foyer.
- Sandwiches and fruit at 6:15 PM. Vegan and halal options are labeled.

## House rules
- Quiet in the hallway until the library closes at 6 PM.
- The room must be reset and empty by 8:45 PM.
