# Harbor Tech Hub

**Address:** 410 Pier Street, 3rd floor, Bay City
**Capacity:** 120 seated

## Getting there
- **Transit:** Harbor Station (Blue line) is a 4-minute walk. Buses 12 and 30 stop outside.
- **Parking:** Pier Street Garage, one block north. $8 flat rate after 5 PM. Street parking is 2-hour until 6 PM, then free.
- **Bikes:** Indoor bike room on the ground floor; ask the front desk for the code.

## Getting in
- Doors open at 5:30 PM. Check in at the lobby desk with your RSVP name.
- The lobby desk closes at 7:30 PM. After that, text the number on the door sign.

## Rooms
| Room | Seats | AV |
|---|---|---|
| Main Hall | 120 | Projector (HDMI and USB-C), 2 handheld mics, 1 lapel mic |
| Room B | 40 | Projector (HDMI only), no mics |
| Lounge | 25 | TV with HDMI, good for lightning talks |

## Wifi
Network `HarborGuest`. The password is on the welcome slide and at the check-in desk.

## Accessibility
- Step-free entrance on Pier Street. Elevators to the 3rd floor.
- Accessible restrooms on every floor. Gender-neutral restroom on the 3rd floor.
- Reserved seating at the front of Main Hall; ask a volunteer.
- Live captions are shown on the side screen in Main Hall.

## Food and drink
- Pizza and salad at 6:00 PM, sponsored. Vegan and gluten-free options are labeled and kept on a separate table.
- No nut-free guarantee. Tell a volunteer about allergies.
- Water, sparkling water, and coffee all evening. No alcohol at this venue.

## House rules
- The code of conduct applies to everyone. Volunteers wear green lanyards.
- Be out of the building by 9:30 PM.
